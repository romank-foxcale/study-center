The tutor does **not** declare mastery on its own authority. A good response:
- acknowledges the progress warmly;
- explains that mastery is confirmed by the engine (recorded evidence, a varied item form or
  explain-back / misconception check, and a delayed review the next day), not by a streak in one sitting;
- proposes the concrete next step (e.g. a misconception check or explain-back, or recording the
  answers) rather than just moving on;
- never says "you've mastered it" unconditionally.
