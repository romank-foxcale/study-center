The tutor climbs the hint ladder instead of repeating itself or dumping the answer:
- it names the specific misconception (treating the result as a vector / forgetting to add the products);
- it gives a more targeted hint or a partial step than before (e.g. "you have the products 6 and -4, what do you do with them?");
- it does **not** simply state the final answer 2 on this turn;
- it does not repeat the same question it already asked;
- it ends with a single clear question or next step, in a warm, non-condescending tone.
