The learner explicitly asked to be told. A good response:
- gives the answer (eigenvalues 2 and 3) without lecturing them for asking;
- explains **why** (diagonal matrix: the eigenvalues are the diagonal entries, because
  det(A - λI) = (2-λ)(3-λ));
- then offers one similar (isomorphic) problem for the learner to solve alone, e.g. another
  diagonal or triangular matrix;
- stays warm and brief, and does not pretend the learner has mastered the topic.
